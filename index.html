Standard Operating Protocol: Baseline Bucket Pre-Dissolve Method

To prevent chemical precipitation, curdling, or nozzle clogging, pre-dissolve each product in its own bucket of clean water before adding to the pump:

  1. Fill spray pump half-full (approx. 7–8 Liters) with clean water.
  2. WP / DF / WG Powders: Dissolve completely in Bucket 1 and pour into pump.
  3. SG / SP Granules & Powders: Dissolve completely in Bucket 2 and pour into pump.
  4. SC / SL / EC Liquids: Mix in Bucket 3 with water and pour into pump.
  5. Fill pump to 15 Liters total water. Finally, add 5 ml Silicone Super-Spreader last (approx. 0.33 ml/L). Stir gently and spray within 1–2 hours.
