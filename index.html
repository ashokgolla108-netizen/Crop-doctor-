Professional Tier-Based & Weather-Responsive Spray Program (All Doses per 15-Liter Pump)

Standard Operating Protocol: Baseline Bucket Pre-Dissolve Method

To avoid chemical curdling, settling, or nozzle clogging, never put raw chemicals directly together in the main pump tank. Follow this step-by-step mixing order:

  1. Fill spray pump half-full (approx. 7–8 Liters) with clean water.
  2. Pre-dissolve each WP / WG powder separately in its own bucket of water before pouring into the tank.
  3. Pre-dissolve SG granules, SP powders, and SL/SC liquids in separate buckets before pouring into the tank.
  4. Fill pump water to 15 Liters, then add 5 ml Silicone Super-Spreader last. Stir gently and spray within 1–2 hours.
